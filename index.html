Notes for the client

  • All three use placeholder imagery (Unsplash) — the look will shift meaningfully once real CVC photography is in. Image swap is mechanical from there.
  • Copy is identical across all three — pulled directly from your refresh brief. Comparing apples to apples on design only.
  • Desktop-first for this round. Mobile/tablet polish comes after a direction is locked.
  • Only Home + Services are mocked. About, Gallery, and Inquiry get built out in the chosen direction once you pick one.
  • Pick one direction (or describe a hybrid — e.g. "B's palette with A's layout") and we'll rebuild it properly in Next.js with real photos, the inquiry form wired up, and full responsive treatment.
