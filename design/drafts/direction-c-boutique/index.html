— The Studio

A full-service practice, built around presence.

Creative Vision Consultancy is a full-service wedding and event management company serving weddings, social, and corporate events. From intimate gatherings to large-scale celebrations, we transform your vision into a seamless, thoughtfully curated experience that feels personal, intentional, and unforgettable.

From planning to execution, our goal is for clients to fully enjoy their day, knowing every detail has been managed and every guest is considered. Based in Atlanta and serving clients nationwide, we approach every event with care, precision, and respect for the story being told.
